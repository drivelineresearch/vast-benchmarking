## 💵 Cost and cleanup

- **$1.202** was the observed Vast account-credit movement during the expansion.
- **$2.852** is the campaign's conservative rental-ledger estimate.
- **$3.439** is the conservative estimate across all repository campaigns.
- The expansion stayed below its authorized **$10** ceiling.
- Every instance created by these campaigns was destroyed and verified absent afterward.

The conservative estimates intentionally exceed observed account movement. They use
recorded rental intervals and rates, while provider billing can apply its own timing and
rounding behavior.

## 🧠 Interpretation notes

**Effective cores are container-visible capacity.** The value is the lower of process
affinity and cgroup CPU quota, and can be fractional. A host advertising 512 cores does
not receive credit for 512 when the rented container can schedule only 64.

**This is a capacity comparison, not a universal hardware review.** Results depend on
the container image, CUDA stack, thermal state, storage path, provider configuration,
and benchmark version. Use the [methodology](../benchmark-methodology.md) before drawing
conclusions outside the tested workloads.
